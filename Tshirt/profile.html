FAQs

What happens when I update my email address (or mobile number)?
Your login email id (or mobile number) changes, likewise. You'll receive all your account related communication on your updated email address (or mobile number).
When will my WaveNexa account be updated with the new email address (or mobile number)?
It happens as soon as you confirm the verification and save the changes.
What happens to my existing WaveNexa account when I update my email address (or mobile number)?
Updating your email address (or mobile number) doesn't invalidate your account. Your account remains fully functional and your 30-day persistent session stays intact. You'll continue seeing your Order history, saved information, and personal details.
Does my order history get affected when I update my email address?
No, all past order records and tracking IDs remain safely linked with your profile.
