💬 Orders Help & FAQs

Can I cancel my order?

Yes! You can cancel your order within 42 hours of placing it directly on this page or from your Profile. After 42 hours, orders enter courier fulfillment.

How do Returns and Replacements work?

Once your order is marked Delivered, return and size exchange buttons become active. We provide free 7-day doorstep pickup across India.

When will my refund be processed?

Wallet refunds are instant. UPI or card payment refunds take 3 to 5 business days to credit back to your bank account.
